The generic QEMU `virt` board defaults to cortex-a76 and does not provide the
M30 GICv2/timer/board handoff. Its undefined-instruction loop and blank
framebuffer are therefore NOT Android/iOS UI evidence and are not used to
accept or reject this candidate. The authoritative checks are CI, source hash,
kernel/platform/buildtree validators, packaging/handoff validators, and the
physical BOOT-only result.

The full trace is retained compressed as qemu.log.gz; `result.json` records
`blank_screen` with `snapshot_only=true`.
