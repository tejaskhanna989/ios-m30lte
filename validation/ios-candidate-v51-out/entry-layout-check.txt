3) Payload layout
-----------------
Payload header fields (mkpayload.py output):
  v50 kernel_end 0xfffffff008450000 -> ADT PA 0x82450000, boot_args PA 0x82454000
  v51 kernel_end 0xfffffff008454000 -> ADT PA 0x82454000, boot_args PA 0x82458000

shim/loader.c computes these from the payload header:
  pa_of_va_base = phys_base - virt_base
  adt_pa        = kernel_end + pa_of_va_base
  ba_pa         = align16k(adt_pa + adt_size)
No hardcoded 0x82450000 or 0x82454000 exists in the shim. MEM_SIZE is
0x3d900000 from phys_base 0x80000000, so both new addresses remain in RAM.

The shim Image/ELF and the shim region in shim-boot.img remain byte-identical
to the v31 reference.
